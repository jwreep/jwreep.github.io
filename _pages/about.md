I'm a solar astronomer at the University of Hawaii's Institute for Astronomy. My research is focused on understanding the transport of energy through the solar atmosphere. I focus primarily on hydrodynamic modeling of the solar corona and solar flares, trying to understand how magnetic reconnection causes the observed variability.
