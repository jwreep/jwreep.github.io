I'm a solar astronomer at the University of Hawaii's Institute for Astronomy, located on the island of Maui. I also teach astronomy courses at the University of Hawaii at Mānoa, in Honolulu.

My research is primarily focused on hydrodynamic modeling of the solar corona and solar flares, trying to understand how the dissipation of magnetic energy causes the observed solar activity. I use model-data comparisons to constrain the underlying physics of the corona, and the transport of energy through all the layers of the solar atmosphere.
